How this map is drawn

  • Generated from plan v2 (source/full-plan/plan_v2.json, APPROVED 2026-11-16). Nothing is planted yet; this is the plan, not a record.
  • Schematic, not surveyed. Beds sit 7.42 ft apart (GreenStar track width); aisles between areas are 3 tracks (~24 ft). A–C south ends are drawn on one line (rows are planted from the north end).
  • Cross aisles (6 ft): A1–A15 one at 132 ft; A16–C at 110 and 226 ft where the bed is long enough. Field D (Nov 21): a 6-ft path 70 ft into the Show Garden side and a 6-ft cross aisle 85 ft into the east part. The east aisle really runs at an angle (it reaches D17-east sooner); it is drawn straight here.
  • D sits north-east of A–C; its exact offset is not recorded. Field D updated Nov 21 from Jeroen's measurements: Show Garden side 266 ft; east part D1 170 ft tapering evenly to D17 130 ft; D18-east and D19-east 100 ft each; no D20. The grass strip between the two parts is drawn 20 ft wide (not measured). Mix positions in the tooltips are usable feet from the start of the east part, aisle not counted, as in column J of the 'Nov 21st mixes' tab.
  • Dashed outlines show where each 50-ft cover lies.
